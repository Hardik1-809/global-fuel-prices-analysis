About The Analytics Project

“Global Fuel Prices and Taxation Trends: A Comprehensive Analysis” bridges empirical energy economics and visual storytelling through Python Flask and Tableau Public.

Foundation

Project Objectives

Energy costs directly impact global supply chains, household disposable income, inflation rates, and geopolitical stability. This project was developed to provide an open, transparent, and interactive mechanism for researchers, policymakers, students, and citizens to analyze international fuel price formation.

  • Track the sensitivity of retail gasoline, diesel, and LPG to Brent crude benchmark trends.
  • Quantify the proportion of pump prices constituted by statutory government taxes versus product cost.
  • Examine how geographic location and World Bank income classification dictate national fuel pricing.
  • Evaluate the shielding effect of government energy subsidies during periods of extreme market volatility.

Process Pipeline

Data Preparation Methodology

  • 1. Currency & Volume Normalization: Global retail fuel prices are frequently quoted in local sovereign currencies and disparate imperial/metric units (e.g., Gallons, Liters). All values were standardized into United States Dollars per Liter (USD/L) based on historical daily exchange rates.
  • 2. Handling Missing Values & Outliers: Anomalous spikes or missing observations caused by holiday reporting halts were treated using forward-fill imputation for temporal consistency without skewing overall variance.
  • 3. Categorical Harmonization: Regional groupings and World Bank Income tiers (Low, Lower-Middle, Upper-Middle, High) were verified against ISO standard alpha-3 sovereign country codes.
  • 4. Tax & Subsidy Segmentation: Tax percentages were decomposed into statutory excise taxes, environmental surcharges, and VAT, while subsidy levels were tiered into High, Moderate, and None.

Academic Integrity

Limitations of the Analysis

  • Exchange Rate Volatility: Converting domestic currency pump prices to USD introduces foreign exchange fluctuation noise during periods of local currency depreciation.
  • Sub-National Intra-Country Variance: National averages do not capture localized price spikes in remote, landlocked, or rural provinces where transport logistics inflate distribution fees.
  • Tax Transparency Lag: Unbundling federal excise versus municipal value-added tax depends on each country's disclosure cadence, which may result in minor reporting latency.
  • Refinery Quality Differentials: Octane ratings (e.g., RON 91 vs RON 95) and sulfur content standards vary across nations and are grouped within standard fuel buckets.
