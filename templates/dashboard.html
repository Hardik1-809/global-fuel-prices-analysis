How to Read This Dashboard
Global Fuel Map

Choropleth map displays retail fuel pricing tiers across sovereign borders. Darker hues represent higher consumer pump costs.

Tax vs. Crude Spread

Correlates Brent crude oil benchmark against national taxation percentages to demonstrate why pump prices diverge from raw crude trends.

Income Bracket Analysis

Categorizes nations into High, Upper-Middle, and Lower-Middle tiers to reflect energy purchasing power parity and affordability burden.

Subsidy Shielding

Highlights governments applying direct or indirect fuel subsidies that cushion domestic populations from international price shocks.
